**Abstract (excerpt).** The spider robot is designed to take on challenging tasks in hazardous conditions. It can move across challenging terrain like walls and rough surfaces, and effectively find lost objects. In this paper, an innovative multi-modal control approach was developed for the Surveillance Spider Robot (SSR) application, integrating voice recognition and hand gesture recognition as control commands. SSR, a six-legged robot, was designed using a Raspberry Pi 4B embedded device, Arduino Uno kit, RC Servo motors (MG996R), 18650 batteries, mini USB microphone (MI-350), Pi camera V1 (OV5647) and PWM generator (PCA9685). The robot can be controlled through voice or hand gesture recognition captured via camera and microphone.
