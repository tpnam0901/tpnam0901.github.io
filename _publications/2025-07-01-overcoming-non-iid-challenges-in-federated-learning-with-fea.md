**Abstract.** Federated Learning (FL) is a pivotal framework for enhancing AI models in healthcare by preserving data privacy during training. However, non-iid (not independent and identically distributed), due to diverse patient demographics and medical diagnostic tools, often hinders model convergence. To address this issue, we propose a novel approach that integrates an angular loss function to align feature representations across clients in angular space. Experimental results demonstrate that this method enhances the overall performance of FL strategies while improving the separation of feature representation of the global model. These findings underscore the potential for privacy-preserving AI applications in medicine.
