**Abstract (excerpt).** Accurate skin disease classification from medical images is challenging due to data complexity. This paper proposes a novel hybrid classical-quantum framework to improve the performance of classical deep learning approaches on the skin lesion classification problem. This research conducts experiments on the HAM10000 dataset, a dataset that comprises skin lesion images for the classification task. To leverage the quantum advancements but still have limitations of the noisy intermediate-scale quantum era, we design a variational quantum circuit (VQC) architecture for enhancing the performance of classical features on HAM10000 dataset. We also compared the performance of some well-known pretrained deep learning architectures to demonstrate the advancement of our proposed hybrid classical-quantum approach.
