**Abstract (excerpt).** Object detection (OD) faces the costly hurdle of large-scale dataset annotation. Active Learning (AL) is a promising solution that selects the most beneficial samples for annotation. Applying AL to OD is challenging, as it requires addressing uncertainties in both classifying and localizing objects while combining object-level information into a single image-level decision. Current methods, like uncertainty sampling, often rely on output-level uncertainty or heuristic signal combinations. However, relying on static model outputs limits their ability to capture underlying feature-space instabilities. To address these limitations, we propose Feature Difficulty-based AL (FDAL), a novel framework that shifts the focus from output-level heuristics to active latent-space manipulation.
