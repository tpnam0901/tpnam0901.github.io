- Yu Qiao, Huy Q. Le, Avi Deb Raha, Phuong-Nam Tran, Apurba Adhikary, Mengchun Zhang, Loc X. Nguyen, Eui-Nam Huh, Dusit Niyato, Choong Seon Hong, <a href="https://doi.org/10.1145/3844947">“A Survey on Foundation Models for Personalized Federated Intelligence”</a>, [ACM Computing Surveys](https://dl.acm.org/journal/csur), 2026  

This survey presents the vision of artificial personalized intelligence (API): adapting foundation models such as LLMs to individual users while keeping their data private. To get there, it proposes personalized federated intelligence (PFI), which combines the privacy benefits of federated learning with the generalization of foundation models. The survey covers efficient personalization at the edge, trustworthy adaptation, and refinement through retrieval-augmented generation.
