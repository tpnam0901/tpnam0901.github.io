- Duong Thanh Tran, Nguyen Doan Hieu Nguyen, Trung Thanh Pham, Phuong-Nam Tran, Thuy-Duong Thi Vu, Cuong Tuan Nguyen, Hanh Dang-Ngoc, Duc Ngoc Minh Dang, <a href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eswa.2024.125437">“SwinTExCo: Exemplar-based video colorization using Swin Transformer”</a>, [Expert Systems with Applications](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/expert-systems-with-applications), vol. 260, 125437, 2025  

SwinTExCo colorizes grayscale videos by transferring colors from a reference (exemplar) image, using a Swin Transformer backbone. It extends our earlier work, Vitexco, presented at ICTC 2023.
